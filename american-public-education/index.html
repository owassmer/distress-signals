An AI agent for sales prospecting

Distress Signals helps brokers and advisers find businesses that may need to hedge against revenue losses. It searches public records for companies where a single decision or disruption outside their control could cost them a significant share of sales. Each proposed deal in HubSpot shows how much is at stake and whom to contact.
