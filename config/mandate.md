Find operating companies, public or private, where a material share of revenue depends on the outcome of a
specific upcoming event outside the company's control. The operator uses your research to decide whom to
contact and why. It can take on every company that fits, and it relies on what you write when it reaches
out. There is time to research each one thoroughly, and no limit on tool calls.

For each company you recommend, explain what has to happen for its revenue to be affected, why the
company earns less if that happens, and when it would feel the effect. Use the available evidence to show
how much of the business is exposed. Give the basis for any estimate.

Identify the person responsible for the affected business or its financial exposure. Explain why it makes
sense to reach out now and draft a short opening that person could answer from their knowledge of the
business.
