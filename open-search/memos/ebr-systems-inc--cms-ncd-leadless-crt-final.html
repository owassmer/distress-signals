02 The company, and how the event reaches its revenue

The company and the revenue

Medical-device company in Sunnyvale, California, listed on the ASX as CHESS Depositary Interests and filing with the SEC. It earns essentially all its revenue from US hospital sales of its one product, the WiSE CRT System, a leadless left-ventricular endocardial pacing system for cardiac resynchronisation therapy, plus small surgical tool kit and battery-replacement sales. FDA approval came on 11 April 2025.

The revenue this memo is about: US net sales of the WiSE CRT System, with related tool kits and battery replacements, recognised when the implant procedure is completed.

Owners and counterparties: Hospitals buy the device and are paid by Medicare (traditional and Medicare Advantage) and commercial insurers. Medicare inpatient and outpatient add-on payments (NTAP from 1 October 2025, transitional pass-through) cover much of the device cost.

How the event reaches the revenue

Most WiSE patients are Medicare beneficiaries: two-thirds of the pivotal-trial participants were 65 or older. Today Medicare coverage of leadless CRT is at MAC discretion, so hospitals can implant traditional-Medicare patients and claim NTAP and pass-through payment without a study. Under the TCET pathway, CMS's proposed NCD 20.41 (18 November 2026) would cover leadless CRT only under Coverage with Evidence Development, and 'not covered for patients outside of a CMS-approved study'. The study must have an active comparator, a 24-month mortality endpoint, ClinicalTrials.gov registration and a protocol delivered to CMS. EBR's planned WiSE-CHOICE study is not yet registered. Once the final NCD takes effect, Medicare implants, and so most of EBR's sales, can go ahead only at sites and for patients enrolled in an approved study. National non-coverage would end Medicare payment. A final NCD covering the device without a study requirement, or a CED with the study approved promptly, would leave sales largely intact. The company expects the NCD to help Medicare Advantage access, an upside not modelled here.

When

The final decision memo is due by 16 February 2027, after the second comment period closes on 18 December 2026. An NCD takes effect on the date of the final decision memo, so its terms govern Medicare WiSE implants for about 10.5 months of 2027. Under CED, Medicare implants would continue only once a CMS-approved study is listed and sites are activated. The case reads whether CMS approves a study by 30 April 2027.

03 The event, and the ways it can turn out

CMS issues final NCD on leadless CRT (WiSE)
Decided · On or by February 16, 2027

CMS posts the final decision memorandum for national coverage analysis CAG-00471N, Leadless CRT - Wireless Stimulation Endocardially (WiSE), on or before 2027-02-16.

Decided by the CMS Coverage and Analysis Group under the TCET pathway. The proposed decision memo of 18 November 2026 proposes coverage under Coverage with Evidence Development (CED), limited to the FDA-labelled 'previously untreatable' and 'high-risk upgrade' patients, inside CMS-approved studies with an active comparator and 24-month all-cause mortality or a mortality-plus-HF-hospitalisation endpoint. The second comment period runs 18 November to 18 December 2026. The statutory final-decision date is 16 February 2027. Proposed memo posted 2026-11-18; public comment closes 2026-12-18; final decision memo expected by 2027-02-16 (the expected completion date on the tracking sheet).[1][2a][2b]

Shown byCMS Medicare Coverage Database, NCA tracking sheet ncaid=322 (CAG-00471N) and final decision memo; NCD Manual section 20.41
Final NCD: leadless CRT covered only under CED
A way it can turn out · Decided · On or by February 16, 2027

The final decision memorandum for CAG-00471N, posted on or before 2027-02-16, covers leadless CRT only for beneficiaries enrolled in a CMS-approved CED study, so that leadless CRT is not covered for patients outside a CMS-approved study (as proposed, with or without changes to the study criteria).[2c]

CMS approves a leadless CRT CED study by 30 Apr 2027
True only with it · Decided · November 23, 2026 – April 30, 2027

By 2027-04-30, CMS lists at least one study of leadless CRT (such as EBR Systems' WiSE-CHOICE study) as a CMS-approved CED study meeting the final NCD 20.41 criteria, on its Coverage with Evidence Development web page.

Decided by CMS's Coverage and Analysis Group, which reviews CED study protocols against the NCD criteria and the AHRQ standards. The proposed criteria require registration on ClinicalTrials.gov and delivery of a complete final protocol to CMS before study start. As of 23 November 2026, EBR's registered studies on ClinicalTrials.gov are WiSE-UP, a totally-leadless feasibility study, SOLVE-CRT follow-up and the WiCS-LV registry, with no WiSE-CHOICE listing. EBR says covered patients would be enrolled in the proposed WiSE-CHOICE study.[3a][2f]

Final NCD: leadless CRT covered without study requirement
A way it can turn out · Decided · On or by February 16, 2027

The final decision memorandum for CAG-00471N, posted on or before 2027-02-16, covers leadless CRT as reasonable and necessary under section 1862(a)(1)(A) for at least the FDA-labelled indications without requiring enrolment in a CMS-approved study.[2d]

Final NCD: leadless CRT nationally non-covered
A way it can turn out · Decided · On or by February 16, 2027

The final decision memorandum for CAG-00471N, posted on or before 2027-02-16, determines that leadless CRT is nationally non-covered for Medicare beneficiaries (outside clinical-trial routine-cost coverage under NCD 310.1).[2e]

Appendix · what the memo rests on

A Findings recorded on the case

inference

As of 23 November 2026, ClinicalTrials.gov lists five EBR Systems WiSE studies. They are the completed WiCS-LV feasibility study, SOLVE-CRT (NCT02922036, active, not recruiting), the WiCS-LV post-market registry, the totally-leadless feasibility study (NCT06561932) and the WiSE-UP utilization study (NCT07098858, recruiting). None is the WiSE-CHOICE study that EBR says would enrol covered Medicare patients. The proposed NCD 20.41 makes ClinicalTrials.gov registration and delivery of a final protocol to CMS conditions of a CMS-approved CED study, and says leadless CRT is not covered outside such a study. So WiSE-CHOICE still has to be registered, approved by CMS and opened at implanting sites before Medicare-covered implants can continue under a final CED decision. That is the gap the case's 'no approved study by 30 April 2027' world models. WiSE-UP is already recruiting and could in principle be adapted, but it is not described as having an active comparator.[7][2c][3a]
