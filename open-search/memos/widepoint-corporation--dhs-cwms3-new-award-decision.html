02 The company, and how the event reaches its revenue

The company and the revenue

Federal technology-management contractor. Most of its revenue comes from managing DHS's mobile devices and wireless carrier services under the Cellular Wireless Managed Services (CWMS) IDIQ, including carrier costs passed through. It also sells identity management and other managed mobility services.

The revenue this memo is about: Revenue from DHS's Cellular Wireless Managed Services program: carrier services, managed service fees and device and accessory reselling to DHS components (CBP, ICE, Coast Guard, FEMA, TSA, USCIS, HQ and others). Billed under CWMS 2.0 task orders and the CWMS 2.5 bridge, and under CWMS 3.0 once it is released.

Owners and counterparties: WidePoint Integrated Solutions Corp. is the operating subsidiary that holds the DHS CWMS 2.0 IDIQ (70RTAC21D00000001), the sole-source CWMS 2.5 bridge IDIQ (70RTAC26D00000006) and the protested CWMS 3.0 IDIQ (70RTAC26D00000003).

How the event reaches the revenue

The CWMS program brought in about 80% of WidePoint's revenue in the first nine months of 2026. DHS awarded the 10-year, roughly $3.1 billion single-award CWMS 3.0 IDIQ to WidePoint on 24 June 2026. GAO sustained TurningPoint Global Solutions' protest on 24 September 2026 and recommended that DHS re-evaluate the proposals, make a new award, and terminate WidePoint's contract for convenience if it chooses someone else. Until DHS decides, WidePoint keeps the business through CWMS 2.0 task orders that run into 2027 and a sole-source CWMS 2.5 bridge 'during protest'. If DHS selects another offeror, DHS components move their task orders to the new awardee, and WidePoint's CWMS revenue ends as its bridge task orders expire or are terminated.

When

The award decision has no announced date. Revised proposals went in during October and November 2026, and the bridge ordering period ran to 4 December 2026, subject to extension. Most bridge task orders are 12 months long and end between August and November 2027, and ICE's bridge order ends 13 January 2027. So a loss would reach revenue a few months after a new award, as components move over. An award by the end of the first quarter of 2027 is assumed to cost about five months of 2027 revenue. An award in the second or third quarter is assumed to cost about the last two months of 2027. An award after September 2027 mostly hits 2028.

Following the GAO's September 2026 decision sustaining TurningPoint Global Solutions' protest (B-424612) of the Cellular Wireless Managed Services (CWMS) 3.0 IDIQ award, the Department of Homeland Security announces a new award decision for CWMS 3.0 on or before 2027-09-30.

Decided by the DHS Office of Procurement Operations after re-evaluating phase one and phase two proposals, as GAO recommended. DHS requested revised proposals after 30 September 2026, and WidePoint has submitted one. Three proposals were received in the first round. DHS has kept WidePoint on a sole-source CWMS 2.5 bridge IDIQ 'during protest', whose ordering period ran 5 August to 4 December 2026, and management says the bridge may be raised or followed by a '2.6 or 2.75' if CWMS 3.0 is delayed further. Appendix · what the memo rests on

A Findings recorded on the case

inference

In USAspending, the WidePoint DHS task orders still running after 23 November 2026 sit on three vehicles. 42 are under CWMS 2.0, ending between 24 November 2026 and 5 August 2027. 44 are under the sole-source CWMS 2.5 bridge, ending between 22 December 2026 and 23 November 2027, and 36 of those end in August to November 2027. One is under the stayed CWMS 3.0 IDIQ. The bridge IDV (ceiling $112,998,271 with options) had its option period one exercised on 16 October 2026 and shows a current end of 4 December 2026. So if DHS re-awards to another offeror, WidePoint's DHS revenue tapers off as these orders end rather than stopping at once. Most of the run-off falls in the third and fourth quarters of 2027, which is the basis for the case's months-lost assumptions. Some large component orders are short: ICE's bridge order 70CTD026FR0000065 runs only 14 September 2026 to 13 January 2027. Those could move to a new awardee earlier.[4a][4b][C1]

SELECT CASE WHEN record_id LIKE '%70RTAC26D00000006%' THEN 'CWMS 2.5 bridge (70RTAC26D00000006)' WHEN record_id LIKE '%70RTAC21D00000001%' THEN 'CWMS 2.0 (70RTAC21D00000001)' WHEN record_id LIKE '%70RTAC26D00000003%' THEN 'CWMS 3.0 (70RTAC26D00000003)' ELSE 'other' END AS parent_vehicle,

FROM usaspending_awards
WHERE recipient LIKE 'WIDEPOINT%' AND awarding_agency='Department of Homeland Security' AND end_date > '2026-11-23'
